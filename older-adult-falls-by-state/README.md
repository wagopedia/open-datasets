# Older-Adult Fall Deaths by State, 2023

**Author:** Moe Kahok (MyElderHome)

## Description

Fall deaths and crude fall-death rates per 100,000 adults aged 65+ for every U.S. state and the District of Columbia in 2023, with the U.S. total. Falls killed 41,400 older adults in 2023 (69.9 per 100,000), and state rates span a more than fivefold range — from 29.5 per 100,000 in Alabama to 158.4 in Wisconsin. Rates in this dataset are crude rates, as published by the CDC.

## Data file

- `older-adult-falls-by-state-2023.csv` — 52 rows (U.S. total + 50 states + District of Columbia): state, fall deaths age 65+ (2023), crude fall-death rate per 100,000 age 65+, multiple of the U.S. rate, rank by rate.

## Source

CDC National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS), Data Brief 532 — fall mortality among adults 65+, 2023 data.
