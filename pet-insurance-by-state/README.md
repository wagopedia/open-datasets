# Pet Insurance by State, 2025

**Author:** Moe Kahok (Wagopedia)

## Description

State-level pet insurance market data for calendar year 2025: each state's share of America's insured pets and its share of total pet-insurance premium (gross written premium), plus Wagopedia's premium-intensity index comparing premium share to insured-pet share. California holds 18.1% of America's insured pets and 19.9% of all premium, while premium per insured pet runs highest in Massachusetts and Connecticut (intensity index 1.18 against a U.S. average of 1.00).

## Data file

- `pet-insurance-by-state-2025.csv` — 51 rows (50 states + District of Columbia): state, share of U.S. insured pets (%), share of U.S. gross written premium (%), premium intensity index, premium band vs U.S. average, source.

## Source

NAPHIA (North American Pet Health Insurance Association), 2026 State of the Industry Report, Section 5 (2025 data). Derived index and banding by Wagopedia.
