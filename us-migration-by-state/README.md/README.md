# Where Americans Moved: U.S. Migration by State, 2022–2023

**Author:** Moe Kahok (MoveNoble)

## Description

State-level rollup of U.S. domestic migration from IRS Statistics of Income data for 2022–2023: inbound, outbound, and net households and adjusted gross income (AGI) for all 50 states and the District of Columbia, covering the 3,966,932 households that moved between states. Texas gained the most households (+56,473), California lost the most (−100,397), and Florida gained the most income, at +$20.65 billion in net AGI.

## Data file

- `movenoble-state-rollup-2022-2023.csv` — 51 rows (50 states + District of Columbia): state, abbreviation, FIPS, inbound households, outbound households, net households, AGI imported (USD), AGI exported (USD), net AGI (USD), average AGI inbound (USD), average AGI outbound (USD).

## Source

Internal Revenue Service, Statistics of Income (SOI) — U.S. Population Migration Data, 2022–2023.

## 2023–2024 edition

State-level rollup of U.S. domestic migration from IRS Statistics of Income data for 2023–2024: inbound, outbound, and net households and adjusted gross income (AGI) for all 50 states and the District of Columbia, covering the 3,810,095 households that moved between states. North Carolina gained the most households (+38,695), California lost the most (−100,911), and Florida gained the most income, at +$15.82 billion in net AGI.

### Source

Internal Revenue Service, Statistics of Income (SOI) — U.S. Population Migration Data, 2023–2024 (released November 20, 2026).
