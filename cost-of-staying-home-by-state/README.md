# The Cost of Staying Home: Long-Term Care Costs by State, 2025

**Author:** Moe Kahok (MyElderHome)

## Description

2025 state medians for long-term care costs in all 50 states plus the U.S. median: non-medical home-caregiver hourly rates, annual home-care cost at 20 and 44 hours per week, assisted living, nursing home (semi-private and private room), and adult day health care — plus the break-even hours per week at which assisted living becomes cheaper than home care. At the national median, full-time home care (44 hrs/week) costs $80,080 a year against $74,400 for assisted living, a break-even of about 41 hours per week.

## Data file

- `cost-of-staying-home-by-state-2025.csv` — 51 rows (United States + 50 states): state, home-caregiver rank, non-medical caregiver hourly rate (USD), home-care annual cost at 20 and 44 hrs/week (USD), assisted living annual (USD), nursing home semi-private and private annual (USD), adult day health care annual (USD), break-even hours per week.

## Source

CareScout Cost of Care Survey 2025 (Genworth/CareScout). Break-even calculation by MyElderHome.
