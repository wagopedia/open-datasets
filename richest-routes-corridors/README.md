# America's Richest Routes: Interstate Migration Corridors, 2022–2023

**Author:** Moe Kahok (MoveNoble)

## Description

All 2,544 interstate migration corridors in the IRS Statistics of Income 2022–2023 data, with households and people moved and the total and average adjusted gross income (AGI) each corridor carried, plus netted two-way corridor pairs and top-25 rankings. New York → Florida is the #1 money corridor at $5.75 billion in total AGI moved, and Illinois → Florida movers carry the highest average income in America at $223,120 per household.

## Data files

- `movenoble-corridors-all-2022-2023.csv` — 2,544 rows: origin and destination state (name, abbreviation, FIPS), households moved, people moved, total AGI (USD), average AGI per household (USD).
- `movenoble-corridors-netted-2022-2023.csv` — 1,273 netted corridor pairs with net households and net AGI toward the gaining state, plus gross flows in both directions.
- `movenoble-corridors-top25-total-agi-2022-2023.csv` — top 25 corridors by total AGI moved.
- `movenoble-corridors-top25-avg-agi-2022-2023.csv` — top 25 corridors by average AGI per household.
- `movenoble-corridors-top25-net-agi-2022-2023.csv` — top 25 netted corridors by net AGI.

## Source

Internal Revenue Service, Statistics of Income (SOI) — U.S. Population Migration Data, 2022–2023.

## 2023–2024 edition

All 2,547 interstate migration corridors in the IRS Statistics of Income 2023–2024 data, with households and people moved and the total and average adjusted gross income (AGI) each corridor carried, plus netted two-way corridor pairs and top-25 rankings.
